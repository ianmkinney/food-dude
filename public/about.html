Cooking, turned up.

AmpliFood is a kitchen companion with a little attitude: graphic, delicious, warm, and built for real-life cooking. Save recipes from anywhere, plan the week, know what's in the cupboard, and ask an AI chef what to make tonight.

What it does

AI recipe import

Paste recipe text or a link, or drop in screenshots from social posts. AmpliFood pulls out the ingredients, steps, and servings for you.

Meal planner

Lay out breakfast, lunch, and dinner for the week and move meals around as plans change.

Pantry with barcode scan

Scan a product's barcode to add it, or type it in. Track quantities and what's about to expire.

Smart grocery lists

Build a list from your meal plan, check things off in the store, and get an AI estimate of what the haul will cost.

AI chef

Ask cooking questions, get recipes from what's already in your pantry, and get step-by-step help while you cook.

Bring your own AI key

AmpliFood doesn't resell AI or run its own AI servers. You connect the provider you already use by pasting your own API key from Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, xAI (Grok), or Google Gemini.

  • Your key stays on your device. On iPhone and Android it's stored in the system's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore, via SecureStore). In the web version it's kept in your browser's storage for this site.
  • Requests go straight to your provider. When you use an AI feature, your device talks directly to the provider you picked. There is no AmpliFood server in between.
  • You stay in control. Your provider bills you for what you use under your own account. Remove the key at any time in Account → Clear.
  • No key? No problem. The recipe book, planner, pantry, and grocery lists all work without one.
