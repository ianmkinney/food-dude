Privacy Policy

The short version: for most people, AmpliFood has no sign-in and no servers that collect your kitchen data. Your recipes and plans stay on your device, and your AI key goes only to the provider you choose.

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Pending Legal review: Owner-only Google sign-in and platform AI (OpenRouter) described below.

Accounts and sign-in

Regular users do not sign in. The name, username, email, and flavor preferences on the Account screen are stored only on your device, as labels for your own use. They are not sent to AmpliFood.

Owner sign-in (app owner only): A hidden Owner sign-in control in Account lets the app owner authenticate with Google. We receive their Google account ID, email address, and display name to verify identity against an allowlist. We keep a short-lived session token on the device and per-day usage counters on the server (request count and estimated tokens). We do not store AI prompts on the server. When the owner uses platform AI, requests go through AmpliFood's server to OpenRouter and the underlying model provider.

Your data stays on your device

  • Recipes, meal plans, pantry items, grocery lists, parties, AI chat history, assistant memory, and your profile are saved in a local SQLite database on your device. In the web version, that database lives in your browser's storage for this site.
  • Ampi memory stores short facts you ask Ampi to remember (likes, household size, equipment, skill, goals). You can search, edit, delete, or turn memory off in Account. Memories are sent to your AI provider only when you allow AI requests, under the same consent as chat.
  • Theme, voice, and display settings are saved locally on your device.
  • We don't upload, back up, or sync any of it. If you delete the app or clear the site's data, it's gone.
  • We don't sell your data. Nothing in this list is used for advertising.

Your AI and voice keys (BYOK)

  • On iPhone and Android, API keys you add (AI provider and optional ElevenLabs voice) are stored with SecureStore in the system's encrypted storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).
  • In the web version, there is no system keychain, so keys are kept in your browser's local storage for this site. Anyone with access to that browser profile could read them, so use Account → Clear on shared computers.
  • Keys are never sent to AmpliFood. Your AI key is sent only with your requests to the AI provider you picked (unless you are the signed-in owner using platform AI via OpenRouter; see above).
  • ElevenLabs (optional): If you add an ElevenLabs API key in Account → Voice, Ampi's spoken replies are synthesized by calling ElevenLabs directly from your device. Reply text is sent to ElevenLabs under your own account; AmpliFood does not receive or store that audio on a server.

What leaves your device, and where it goes

  • AI requests (BYOK) go directly to your chosen provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, or Google Gemini), and only after you've allowed AI requests in the app and use an AI feature. You can turn that permission off in settings. A request can include the text, links, screenshots, photos, PDFs, and other recipe files you attach in chat, and, for AI chef features, relevant pantry items, recipes, and flavor preferences. PDF text is extracted on your device when possible before anything is sent. That provider's own privacy policy and terms govern what it does with the request.
  • Owner platform AI (owner sign-in only): prompts pass through AmpliFood's server to OpenRouter and the selected model provider; prompts are not stored on our server.
  • Party emails (optional): if you use Party invite or sync and are signed in as the app owner (or an allowlisted tester), AmpliFood's server sends email through Resend to addresses you choose. Signed link parties keep data in the URL fragment on your device (not on our servers). Without owner sign-in or Resend configuration, the app opens your own email app instead.
  • Live party sync (optional): when a party owner enables live sync, we store the party name, optional cover photo, member display names (and optional emails you provide), and meal names/descriptions in Neon Postgres (via Vercel) so members can poll for updates. Recipe lists in meals reference IDs on each member's device; your recipes, pantry, and grocery data are not uploaded. Join links use an unguessable token; each member receives a secret token stored on their device.
  • Barcode numbers go to Open Food Facts, a public product database, so the app can look up the product name and details. Only the barcode number is sent, not your profile.
  • Recipe links you import are fetched by your device so the recipe can be read.
  • Web version: the site is hosted on Vercel, which, like any web host, receives standard request information (such as IP address and browser type) when it serves the pages. AmpliFood doesn't add tracking on top of that.

Camera and photos

  • The camera is used only to scan product barcodes when you add or edit pantry items. Frames are read on your device, and only the decoded barcode number is used.
  • When you add recipe screenshots or a recipe photo, AmpliFood uses the system photo picker. The app receives only the photos you select, never your whole library.
  • Microphone: used only while you talk to Ampi with the mic button. In the iPhone and Android apps, speech is turned into text on your device and isn't recorded or saved. On the web, your browser's speech service does the recognition, and it may send the audio to Apple (Safari) or Google (Chrome). AmpliFood never receives or stores the audio, only the resulting text.
  • AmpliFood doesn't use your location.

Consumer health data

Some information you can add, like allergies, diet needs, and flavor preferences, may count as consumer health data under laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act.

  • Why: only to tailor recipe suggestions and to flag recipes that may contain your allergens.
  • Where it's kept: on your device. AmpliFood doesn't receive it.
  • Who else sees it: no one, unless you turn on AI and choose to include your allergies and diet needs with AI requests. Then it's sent to the AI provider you picked, with those requests. The on-device allergen check works either way.
  • Never sold, never used for ads.
  • Your control: edit or delete it any time in Account → Allergies & diet. Turn off including it with AI requests in Account → AI & privacy.

No analytics, no ads

AmpliFood contains no analytics or tracking SDKs, no advertising, and no ad identifiers. We don't sell or share personal data, because we don't collect it.

Children

AmpliFood isn't directed at people under 18 and isn't intended for use by anyone under 18.

Deleting your data

  • Remove an AI key: Account → AI provider → Clear.
  • Remove an ElevenLabs key: Account → Voice → Remove key.
  • Remove everything: delete the app, or in the web version, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions? See Support.
