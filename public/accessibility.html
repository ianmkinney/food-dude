Accessibility

Last reviewed: [date TODO (Ian)]

We want everyone to be able to cook with AmpliFood. We're working toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA across the iPhone, Android, and web versions.

What works today

  • Dark mode. AmpliFood follows your device's light or dark setting, and you can switch it in the app.
  • Reduce Motion. When Reduce Motion is on, AmpliFood turns off its decorative animations.
  • Text scaling. Text follows your device's font size setting.

What we're still fixing

  • Some icon-only buttons don't have spoken labels yet.
  • Some text fields rely on placeholder text instead of a proper label.
  • Orange text and buttons don't yet meet the 4.5:1 contrast ratio in light mode.
  • Screen readers don't yet announce new AI and Ampi replies automatically.

Report a barrier

If something in AmpliFood is hard or impossible to use, tell us at [support email TODO (Ian)]. Please include your device, the assistive technology you use (for example VoiceOver, TalkBack, or a screen magnifier), and where you got stuck. We'll reply within [5] business days.
