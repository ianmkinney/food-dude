Common questions

The AI features say "Add an API key in Account"

AmpliFood uses your own AI key. Open Account, choose Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, or Gemini, paste your key, and save. The recipe book, planner, pantry, and grocery lists work without a key.

Barcode scanning doesn't start

Allow camera access for AmpliFood when asked. In a browser, scanning needs a secure (https) page and a camera; you can always type the product name instead.

"AmpliFood is open in another tab"

In the web version, your kitchen can only be open in one browser tab at a time. Close the other tab, then press Try again.

Where is my data? Can I move it to another device?

Everything is stored on the device (or browser) you use. There's no account or cloud sync yet, so data doesn't move between devices. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Remove your AI key in Account → Clear. To remove everything, delete the app, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.
